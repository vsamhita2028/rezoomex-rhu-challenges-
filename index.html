01 CHALLENGE ONE

AI Accuracy Challenge

A technical twist on two truths and a lie, grounded in product context.

◷ 60–90 seconds

THE OBJECTIVE

Spot the statement that is incorrect or unsupported by the given requirements.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1

    Review a prepared project scenario and three statements about it.

  2. 2

    Identify the one statement that is inaccurate or unsupported. Two are correct.

  3. 3

    A volunteer reveals the answer against a verified reference and connects it to requirements and AI-output validation.

EXAMPLE SCENARIOSPOT THE OUTLIER

Login feature

Users sign in with email and password. Accounts lock after five failed attempts, and passwords can be reset through a registered email.

AUsers can reset their password through their registered email.
BThe account locks after five consecutive failed login attempts.
CUsers can sign in using their mobile number and OTP.
Correct answer: C
Mobile OTP login is not part of the defined requirements.

WHAT PARTICIPANTS LEARN

How to distinguish stated requirements from unsupported assumptions and why verification matters.

REZOOMEX CONNECTION

Opens a conversation about project context, traceability, and validating AI-generated outcomes.

BOOTH SETUP

Prepared scenario and verified answer, displayed or printed; one volunteer to facilitate.

02 CHALLENGE TWO

Feature Assembly Challenge

A hands-on matching activity that brings the pieces of a feature together.

Play the game
◷ 2–3 minutes

THE OBJECTIVE

Select and arrange the right elements to define a coherent software feature.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1

    Receive shuffled persona, requirement, user story, acceptance criteria, and test case cards, including distractors.

  2. 2

    Select the relevant cards and arrange them in a logical feature workflow.

  3. 3

    A volunteer checks the prepared reference solution and discusses structured product and development workflows.

EXAMPLE SOLUTIONBUILD THE FEATURE

Password reset

Arrange these five cards in a logical workflow:

01

PersonaRegistered application user.

02

RequirementAllow users to securely reset forgotten passwords.

03

User storyAs a registered user, I want to reset my password using my registered email so that I can regain account access.

04

Acceptance criteriaA time-limited reset link is sent to the registered email.

05

Test caseVerify that an expired reset link is rejected.

DISTRACTOR CARDS

Reset without identity verification · Send the link to any entered email · Keep links valid indefinitely

WHAT PARTICIPANTS LEARN

How personas, requirements, stories, acceptance criteria, and tests relate to one another.

REZOOMEX CONNECTION

Introduces a discussion about project context and structured requirements workflows.

BOOTH SETUP

Shuffled physical cards or a simple digital interface, a reference solution, and one volunteer.
